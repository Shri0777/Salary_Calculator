Understanding Salary Components

💰

Gross Salary

Your total monthly earnings before any deductions. Includes basic salary, HRA, allowances, and bonuses.

📉

Deductions

Amounts deducted from your gross salary, including PF, professional tax, income tax estimates, and other deductions.

✅

Net Salary

Your take-home pay after all deductions. This is the amount that gets deposited into your bank account.

How It Works

  1. Enter Earnings: Provide your basic salary and any allowances (HRA, bonuses, etc.)
  2. Add Deductions: Enter your monthly deductions like PF, professional tax, and income tax
  3. Calculate: Our tool uses precise Decimal arithmetic to compute your salary breakdown
  4. Review Results: See your gross salary, total deductions, net salary, and annualized estimates
  5. Save: Save any calculation to history for future reference
